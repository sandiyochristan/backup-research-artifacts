=== Analysis ===

The identical zero-permission app (com.vrp.zeroperm, uid 10397, no <uses-permission> declared
at all) is blocked by the OS when directly targeting BugIntentDialogActivity (which requires
android.permission.DUMP), but succeeds completely when routing through the unprotected sibling

attributed to "uid 10356 (com.google.android.apps.betterbug)" -- BetterBug's OWN identity, not

("CoreBackPreview: Window{... BugIntentDialogActivity}"). This is because DispatchActivity's
own startActivity() call to relaunch BugIntentDialogActivity is attributed by the OS to the
CALLING PROCESS (BetterBug itself, which legitimately holds DUMP), not to whoever originally
launched DispatchActivity.
